2. Information we store

Based on the Bot’s current stated storage design, the following categories may be stored:

  • Discord user IDs: identifiers used to recognize a user and associate that user with Bot features or settings.
  • Discord usernames: names used to identify or display users in relevant Bot features.
  • Discord server IDs: identifiers used to distinguish servers and associate settings with the correct server.
  • Configuration settings: settings saved by server owners or administrators so configured Bot features can operate.

The Bot may also process information included in a Discord interaction or event while responding to a command or carrying out a feature. Processing information to respond does not necessarily mean it is saved in a persistent database. The Bot operator should review the deployed code, database, logs and hosting configuration to confirm exactly what is retained.

We do not ask users to provide Discord passwords, account authentication codes or payment card details to use the Bot. Never send these secrets to the Bot or its operator.

3. How we use information

We use the information listed above only as reasonably necessary to:

  • identify users and servers when a Bot feature requires it;
  • save and apply server-specific configuration;
  • respond to commands and interactions and provide the Bot’s features;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and protect the Bot against misuse or security issues; and
  • respond to support, privacy or deletion requests.

4. Legal bases and permissions

Where privacy laws require a legal basis, the operator will process information on an appropriate basis for providing the Bot, operating and securing its features, complying with legal obligations, or acting on a user’s request or consent where applicable. The operator will only use Discord data in ways permitted by Discord’s Developer Terms and applicable law.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be made available only where needed to operate the Bot or in the following circumstances:

  • Discord: the Bot operates on Discord and exchanges data with Discord through Discord’s APIs and services.
  • Service providers: infrastructure, hosting, database or technical providers may process information on the operator’s behalf when necessary to run and maintain the Bot.
  • Legal or safety reasons: information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security or the integrity of the service.
  • Your direction: information may be shared when you specifically ask us to do so.

We do not authorize service providers to use Bot data for their own unrelated purposes. Their handling of information may also be subject to their own privacy terms.

6. Storage, security and international transfers

Information may be stored in the Bot’s configured database, files or hosting environment. The exact provider and storage region depend on the operator’s deployment setup. Because Discord and infrastructure providers may operate in different countries, information may be processed outside your country.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the Bot’s operation. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Access to stored information should be limited to people who need it to operate or support the Bot.

7. How long we keep information

We keep stored information only for as long as it is needed for the Bot’s stated features, operational security or a legal obligation. When information is no longer needed, when the Bot stops operating, or when a valid deletion request is received, we will take steps to delete it, unless the law requires continued retention. The Bot’s operator should also configure backups and logs so that deleted data is not kept longer than necessary.

Removing the Bot from a Discord server may not automatically erase every saved configuration record. Contact us to request deletion of server-related data.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you are authorized to make the request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. A request may be limited where an exception under applicable law applies.

9. Children’s privacy

The Bot is not intended for anyone who is below the minimum age required to use Discord or below the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service. If you believe a child has provided information to the Bot, contact us so we can review the request.

The Bot relies on Discord. The policy for Discord’s own services is available at Discord’s Privacy Policy. This Policy applies to the Bot operator’s handling of information and does not replace Discord’s policies or the policies of hosting and infrastructure providers.
